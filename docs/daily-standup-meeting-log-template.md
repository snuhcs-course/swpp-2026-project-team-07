# Daily Standup Meeting Log — Template

This consolidates the team's Iteration 1 topic-based notes and to-do lists under the six headings in the existing Notion meeting template. The action table keeps the kickoff notes' requirements, deadlines and owners together.

Keep one Wiki page titled **Daily Standup Meeting Log**, group entries by iteration, and append a dated entry after each meeting. Keep previous entries as the record of that meeting; record later progress in the next entry. Use `Not recorded` for missing source details and `Not assigned` only when the team explicitly left a task unassigned.
