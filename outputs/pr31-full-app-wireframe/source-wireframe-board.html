01 / Navigation

One app. Seven screens.

Home is the presentation library. Setup, playback, corrections, assessments, and saved Q&A are nested views within this navigation. Text in brackets is a wireframe placeholder; charts and waveforms are schematic.

Record → review → improveThe first saved take opens Full Review. Later saved takes open Slide Review. A redo can first open Compare; Keep current or Overwrite resolves the selected recording.
Return paths keep their contextFull Review returns to Home, slides, or Slide Review according to where it opened. Slide Review returns to Full Review when opened there. Q&A returns to Slide Review.
